**Principle 1: Segmentation**  
**Definition:** Segmentation involves dividing a system or object into independent parts and combining them in a more suitable way.  
**Keyword:** Divide [divide, apart, partition, disunit, disassemble, classify, separate]  

---

**Principle 2: Taking Out**  
**Definition:** To improve end-of-life outcomes, eliminate components (e.g., hazardous materials) that reduce reuse, recyclability, or disposal by removing them from the product with minimal energy.  
**Keyword:** Remove [expel, oust, eject, evacuate, dislodge, snatch]  

---

**Principle 3: Local Quality**  
**Definition:** Reduce mental inertia by addressing overly homogeneous and continuous spaces and materials. From an Ecodesign perspective, this principle optimizes processes, thereby reducing energy and mass impacts. It also encourages the creation of multi-functional objects to exploit synergies among functions.  
**Keyword:** Optimize, Material [add, increment, pile, reduce, incremental, improve, adapt, modify, eliminate]  

---

**Principle 5: Merging**  
**Definition:** Offer synergies by combining several machines that share the same energy and control sources.  
**Keyword:** Merge [synergy, combine, union, together, harmony]  

---

**Principle 6: Universality**  
**Definition:** Enable a part or object to perform multiple functions, eliminating the need for additional parts.  
**Keyword:** Universal [multifunction, diverse, multipurpose, all-around, versatile, adaptable, accommodate]  

---

**Principle 13: The Other Way Around**  
**Definition:** Reverse the normal procedure or process. Invert the actions used to solve the problem (e.g., instead of cooling an object, heat it). Make movable parts fixed and fixed parts movable, turn the entity upside down, nest objects inside others, or exploit other dimensions.  
**Keyword:** Reversal [inverse, contrary, improve, reverse, optimize]  

---

**Principle 15: Dynamics**  
**Definition:** A key principle to ensure the system operates only when needed (Operative Time) and always optimally. Adjust the characteristics of an object, external environment, or process to achieve optimal conditions.  
**Keyword:** Optimize, System [efficient, adapt, adjust, modify, acclimatize, conform, comply, distribute]  

---

**Principle 22: Blessing in Disguise**  
**Definition:** Use harmful factors (particularly harmful environmental effects) to achieve a positive outcome.  
**Keyword:** Repurpose [purify, recycle, disinfect, reuse, eliminate]

**Principle 31: Porous Material**  
**Definition:** Improve performance by reducing mass. Focus on the topology or distribution of mass within the object’s volume, arranging mass only where needed and eliminating non-functional portions.  
**Keyword:** Lighten [repurpose, eliminate, distribute, reduce, weight]  

---

**Principle 35: Parameter Change**  
**Definition:** Change an object’s physical state (e.g., gas, liquid, solid), pressure, concentration, consistency, flexibility, or temperature.  
**Keyword:** Changing [transform, alter, convert, modify, evolve]  

---

**Principle 36: Phase Transitionwhats**  
**Definition:** Utilize phenomena occurring during phase transitions, such as volume changes or heat absorption/loss.  
**Keyword:** Transition [volume, absorption]
